Week 7 · Algebraic retrieval

Rank by meaning, not by the exact words someone happened to type.

Every document is a column of a TF-IDF document-term matrix. Your query becomes a vector in the same space and every document is ranked by the cosine of the angle between them. LSI first compresses the matrix into a few concepts with SVD, so overnight, Sunday and after hours point the same way even though they are different words.
